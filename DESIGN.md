# Design

## JIN-SPA website

### Direction

- Mode: Persuade. Help visitors find a relevant service, understand the Bellevue location, and call with questions or to arrange an appointment.
- World: Eastside field guide. The site borrows the legibility and wayfinding of a regional field guide, not another spa template: map-paper neutrals, evergreen landmarks, warm route markers, fine contour linework, and an editorial guide type system.
- First viewport: a clear JIN-SPA masthead and Bellevue locator lead into a spacious welcome with one real spa photograph, a short path into service families, and a direct call action.
- Visitor path: home guide → service family → individual treatment → pricing or contact. Address, daily hours, and phone remain easy to find across the site.
- Signature interaction: restrained route-line movement that leads from the welcome to the service index; all motion is optional and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Cross-surface reach: service pages use the same field-guide masthead, page title plate, section wayfinding, service links, and contact footer. Dense pricing and policy pages retain clear reading measures and contrast.
- Honest risk: the map language must remain a quiet navigation aid and never make service finding feel like a game.

### Visual system

- Palette: map-paper ivory `#e9e4d7`, deep evergreen `#25372f`, moss `#758568`, and restrained brass `#c0a66e`, with dark ink for readable text.
- Materials: uncoated map paper, fine contour linework, botanical and treatment photography already present in the project, and discreet wayfinding rules.
- Type: expressive editorial serif for titles paired with a highly legible sans serif for navigation, service details, prices, and contact information.
- Composition: one strong welcome image, generous editorial measure, numbered map-like service markers only where they genuinely identify a route, and open lists rather than stacks of identical cards.
- Controls: plainly labeled, keyboard-visible links and buttons. Phone is the primary direct-contact action; no online booking link or button.
- Responsive behavior: the service index and detail pages reflow in a single column on narrow screens; navigation remains available; no horizontal page overflow.

### Content and behavior

- Use JIN-SPA, Bellevue, the confirmed address, hours, phone, and services in PRODUCT.md.
- Preserve existing service content and pricing; do not invent offers, reviews, outcomes, or business claims.
- Keep service-directory links and cross-page navigation functional.
- Do not copy any other website's copy, images, brand, or distinctive page composition.
- The local open/closed label uses America/Los_Angeles time; visible daily hours remain present.
